TankGauge — Phone Install Card

Works outdoors by your tank even with your PC switched off. Zero App Store, zero monthly fees.

https://eric1ramaz-sys.github.io/tank-gauge/

Android (Redmi / Chrome)

  1. Point your phone camera at the QR code above and tap the link.
  2. In Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu at top-right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen → Install.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Point the iPhone camera at the QR code and open in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

30-Second Quick Start

  1. Open TankGauge and tap Try Sample Data if you want to explore the graphs and advisor indoors first.
  2. Tap Settings ⚙️ to confirm your tank size (or use Bills → Tank Size Helper) and enter your UK postcode for free weather overlay.
  3. Head out to your Calor tank, tap Snap Dial, align the ring around the dial, confirm the glowing needle line, and log your last Calor bill!
